# ClinicalMate

ClinicalMate is a healthcare support application designed to help healthcare professionals organize clinical information and support day-to-day clinical workflows.

> **Important:** ClinicalMate is not a substitute for professional medical judgment, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency care. Healthcare professionals must independently verify all information and make decisions according to local clinical guidelines, patient circumstances, and applicable regulations.

## Intended Use

ClinicalMate is intended as a clinical workflow and decision-support tool for qualified healthcare professionals.

It must not be used as the only basis for:

- Diagnosing a medical condition
- Selecting or changing treatment
- Prescribing medication
- Managing emergencies
- Replacing consultation with a qualified clinician

Always follow your institution’s policies and current local or national clinical guidelines.

## Safety and Privacy

Do **not** upload, commit, or share real patient-identifiable information in this repository.

Before deploying ClinicalMate in a real healthcare setting, ensure that you have reviewed:

- Patient consent and confidentiality requirements
- Local health-data protection laws and institutional policies
- User authentication and access controls
- Encryption for data in transit and at rest
- Audit logging and backup procedures
- Clinical validation of calculators, recommendations, and medical content
- Appropriate procedures for urgent or high-risk clinical findings

This project may contain demonstration or test data only. Any sample patient data must be fictional and non-identifiable.
